## Questions

For a non-sensitive question, open a GitHub issue and choose **Support request**. Name the module, AI coding tool, version, goal, and observed behavior.

For direct contact, email [hoc@wi.works](mailto:hoc@wi.works).

For the human-layer introduction, visit [d.wi.works](https://d.wi.works).

## Security

Do not put secrets or sensitive reports in an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Boundaries

Support does not guarantee compatibility with every model, harness, plugin, policy, or environment. Dwi does not provide emergency, legal, medical, or financial services.
