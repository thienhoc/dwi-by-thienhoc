# Community conduct

Dwi is built around the idea that human attention deserves system design. The community should reflect that.

## Expected

- Be clear without being cruel.
- Ask answerable questions and define unfamiliar terms.
- Critique behavior, evidence, and tradeoffs rather than a person's worth.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, attribution, and a person's right to stop.
- Correct a concrete mistake briefly and without asking the affected person to provide emotional reassurance.
- Make room for newcomers while preserving technical precision.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or doxxing
- Publishing private messages, credentials, personal data, or security details without permission
- Manipulative apology, guilt, pressure, or claims that a person owes continued participation
- Misrepresenting evidence, authorship, endorsement, compatibility, or safety
- Repeatedly expanding another contributor's scope after they have set a boundary

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, email [hoc@wi.works](mailto:hoc@wi.works) with the relevant links, dates, and the outcome you need. Do not include unrelated private content.

The maintainer may remove content, limit participation, or close a thread to protect people and the project. Reports will be handled as privately as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.
